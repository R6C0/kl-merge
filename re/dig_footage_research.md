## Campaign "Reunion" beat by beat
Text walkthroughs (no timestamps; fetch of video pages gave only titles):
1. Start: Kane and Lynch climb out of the excavation hole; Kane executes the guard on his left (E) and takes his pistol; Lynch is held by another guard.
   Wife already executed by Mute (cutscene); Kane beat Mute; Jenny hides in a small ditch/pit; a van stops on a small hill nearby. (portforward, gamepressure p1)
2. Objective "protect Jenny": four waves of The7 men (mercenaries in vans, not police). Wave 1 slow, frontal, from the ramp near the backhoe/excavator;
   wave 2 from a parking-garage-like structure right of the pit, spawns close; wave 3 from the left of the pit, central area, little cover; final wave
   arrives in two vans, hardest, split left (Kane) / right (Lynch). Cover: concrete pillars, the van. Weapons: pistol, SMG drops.
3. Cutscene with Jenny; objective "Stop the truck - kill the driver": a large dump truck drives over the heroes' position (stay put in cover, then turn).
4. Dump truck: guards ride in the back (one dies in the accident); truck turns around; shoot the side window and cab until the driver dies;
   last guard sits in the truck, go round and kill him. Mission ends (next: ch.8 Breakout). Checkpoint reload if the truck is not stopped.
5. Crane: gamepressure overview claims "disable a large crane" at the end; parts 1 and 2 do NOT mention it. Unconfirmed, treat as unreliable.
6. Music mood: not documented in text sources; needs a video listen (not done).

## Beats that suit an FA round
- Start in the pit, climb the ramp (spawn); guard waves from ramp / garage structure / left / rear = AI squads with timed arrival (use DD spawn cops).
- Two vans of reinforcements = cop/guard cars arriving with SWAT cells (same system as DM02 mall police cars).
- Excavator/backhoe, concrete pipes, scaffolding, container: cover and loot spots (briefcases in the container, on the scaffolding).
- Dump truck set-piece: scripted truck pass at a timer (kills anyone in the lane) = hazard; escape could be that truck or the van on the hill.
- Not suited: Jenny escort, driver-shoot objective, cutscenes.
